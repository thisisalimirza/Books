# Outline

Structure first. Each entry says what the chapter *does* for the argument,
not just what it covers. This is a first pass — expect it to move.

## Part One — The Problem

### Chapter — The Good Intention, Traced to the Bad Outcome

- **Does:** opens with a single concrete policy and follows the causal
  chain from its stated goal to the patient-level harm it produced
- **Covers:** the case study; establishes the book's throughline
- **Ends on:** the question — was this predictable?

### Chapter — Why Harm Hides

- **Does:** explains the structural reasons patient harm from policy is
  hard to see in advance (diffusion, delay, who bears the cost vs. who
  decides)
- **Covers:** incentive misalignment, measurement gaps, whose outcomes get
  counted
- **Ends on:** setting up the need for a predictive tool

## Part Two — The Pattern

### Chapter — Case Studies

- **Does:** builds the evidence base across multiple policies/domains
- **Covers:** 3-5 policy case studies, each showing the same causal shape
- **Ends on:** the common structure across cases

## Part Three — The Index

### Chapter — Building the Policy Patient Index

- **Does:** lays out the methodology — what the index measures, how it's
  scored, what data it needs
- **Covers:** index components, validation against the case studies from
  Part Two
- **Ends on:** the index in action

### Chapter — Applying the Index

- **Does:** runs the index against current or pending policy to show it
  works prospectively, not just as retrospective explanation
- **Covers:** worked examples, limitations, what the index can't catch

## Part Four — What Changes

### Chapter — Toward Patient-Impact Review

- **Does:** argues for institutionalizing something like this index —
  who should require it, and what would change if they did
- **Covers:** policy recommendations, precedent (environmental/fiscal
  impact review models)
- **Ends on:** the call to action
