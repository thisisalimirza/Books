# Name Change

I’m renaming “Thinking in Public” to “Side Effects.”

Everyone talks about the thing. I want to talk about the thing after the thing.

The side effects. The unintended consequences. The second-order impacts that actually matter, and how you can capitalize on them today. Early.

That’s what I’ve been writing about for 10 months - I just didn’t have a name for it.

If you’re still here, you’re probably someone trying to build something while pursuing a traditional career path (med school, law school, PhD, consulting, banking, engineering.) You’re resource-constrained. You refuse to be put in one box. You want to understand how things *actually* work, not just how they’re supposed to work

Side Effects is for you.

Let’s go.
