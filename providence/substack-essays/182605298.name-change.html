I’m renaming “Thinking in Public” to “Side Effects.”

Everyone talks about the thing. I want to talk about the thing after the thing.

The side effects. The unintended consequences. The second-order impacts that actually matter, and how you can capitalize on them today. Early.

That’s what I’ve been writing about for 10 months - I just didn’t have a name for it.
