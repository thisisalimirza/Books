# About the Author

**Ali Mirza** is a medical student and builder. Before medical school he
worked at Epic, the health software company whose systems run most American
hospitals, and ran JANUS, a services business he shut down to begin training.

He is the founder of Rounds, a medical education platform built around a gap
he noticed in his own studying: modern training drills enormous volumes of
discrete facts and rarely teaches the connective understanding that holds
them together.

He writes at blog.thisisalimirza.com about the intersection of medicine,
technology, and incentives. He was born in Pakistan and lived in Lebanon and
Kenya before moving to the United States at twelve.

*This is his first book.*
