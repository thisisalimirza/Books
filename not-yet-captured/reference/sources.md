## Ch11 — Midjourney scanner

**verified.** Ahn HS, Kim HJ, Welch HG. "Korea's Thyroid-Cancer 'Epidemic'
— Screening and Overdiagnosis." *N Engl J Med* 2014;371:1765–1767.
Thyroid cancer diagnoses in 2011 were 15× the 1993 rate; mortality stable;
cancers <2cm accounted for 94.4% of the increase. Driven by fee-for-service
providers adding ultrasound to government-funded screening.

**verified.** Ahn HS, Welch HG. "South Korea's Thyroid-Cancer 'Epidemic' —
Turning the Tide." *N Engl J Med* 2015. Documents the pullback in screening
and thyroidectomy.

**partial.** Post-pullback mortality among those diagnosed after 2015. The
seed essay claimed mortality "crept back up." Not confirmed. The chapter has
been softened to say the question is contested. **Do not restore the stronger
claim without a source.**

**verified, with correction.** USPSTF 2012 grade D recommendation against
PSA screening; revised 2018 to shared decision-making for men 55–69.
Post-2012: PSA testing and biopsy rates fell nationally; multiple analyses
found higher-stage disease and rising metastatic disease at diagnosis.
Mortality had been declining pre-2012 and **remained steady** afterward —
it flattened, it did not rise. No causal relationship established.
See *JAMA Netw Open* 2022 on the grade D recommendation and
prostate-cancer-specific mortality.
**The draft originally said mortality "flattened or turned back upward."
That overstated the evidence and has been corrected.**

**unverified.** Inova Health System firefighter cancer screening program
using whole-body MRI/ultrasound. Referenced as the model for studying an
immature screening technology in an enriched-risk population. Needs a
primary source.

**unverified.** Midjourney/Butterfly Network licensing terms, the ~50,000
unit target, and current regulatory posture. Fast-moving; re-check near
publication.

## Ch12 — When Does Technology Kill a Profession

**verified.** Jevons WS. *The Coal Question*, 1865.

**verified, with correction.** Bessen J. *Learning by Doing*, and "Toil and
Technology," IMF *Finance & Development*, March 2015. ATMs reduced tellers
per branch from ~21 to ~13; urban branch counts rose ~43%; total teller
employment rose from roughly 500,000 in the early 1980s to nearly 600,000 by
2010, across a period when ~400,000 ATMs were installed.
**The draft originally cited "268,000 in 1970 to 608,000 by 2006," a figure
that circulates in retellings and does not match Bessen's series. Corrected.**

**unverified.** Teller employment falling from 332,000 FTE in 2010 to
~164,000 by 2022. Directionally consistent with the mobile-banking argument
but the specific figures need a BLS series check.

**verified, with caveat.** Vancouver Workload Utilization Evaluation Study,
*J Am Coll Radiol* 2013: direct image interpretation 36.4% of radiologist
time; non-interpretive work 43.8%; total clinical productivity 87.7%; an
average of six interactions per hour with other clinical staff, 81.2% of
which influenced patient care in real time. **Caveat:** the figure is
methodology-dependent — a later interruption-focused study found 52% spent
interpreting. The chapter now states both and rests the argument only on the
claim both support, which is that a large share of the work is
non-interpretive.

**unverified.** UK 40% consultant radiologist shortfall by 2028 (RCR
workforce census) and US physician shortfall projections (AAMC).

**unverified.** FDA autonomous-diagnosis clearance status. Time-sensitive;
verify at typesetting.

**verified as attribution.** Christensen C. *The Innovator's Prescription* —
the new-visibility-creates-specialties argument.

**unverified.** GP share of US physicians: 84% in 1931 vs ~25% in 2019.
**unverified.** Lillehei cross-circulation, first successful open-heart
operation 1954, University of Minnesota.

## Ch8 — Statistics

**partial.** Statins in primary prevention: TheNNT gives NNT 104 to prevent
one nonfatal MI and 154 to prevent one stroke over five years. In higher-risk
hyperlipidemic men the five-year NNT falls to roughly 40–70. **The draft
originally said "one to three hundred" for one heart attack, which was too
loose; corrected to roughly one hundred, with the risk-dependence stated in
text.** Two downstream sentences that referenced the old range have been
brought into line.

**unverified.** The remaining calibration figures — strep antibiotics,
post-stroke BP medication, aspirin during acute MI. The chapter now gives
these as approximate magnitudes rather than precise numbers, since the spread
is what the argument needs. Pin them down against TheNNT before typesetting
if precise figures are wanted.

## Ch4 — Turn the Volume Up

**verified (the science).** Sjoding MW, Dickson RP, Iwashyna TJ, Gay SE,
Valley TS. "Racial Bias in Pulse Oximetry Measurement." *N Engl J Med*
2020;383:2477–2478. Black patients had nearly three times the frequency of
occult hypoxemia undetected by pulse oximetry. Among readings of 92–96%,
arterial saturation was below 88% in 12% of measurements in Black patients
versus 4% in white patients. Cited directly in the chapter now rather than
through a secondary account.

warned by advisors. Needs a primary interview or published profile.
